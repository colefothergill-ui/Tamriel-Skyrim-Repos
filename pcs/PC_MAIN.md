## Stunts
1) **Thu'um Prodigy:** Because Eltric is a gifted user of the Thu'um, he gains **+2** when using **Will** to roll for Shouts (4dF).
2) **Nobleman's Diplomacy:** Because Eltric is an educated noble of Falkreath Hold, he gains **+2 Rapport** when addressing **Jarls, Housecarls, Stewards, and aristocratic Nords** in formal situations.
3) **Stag-Rush CHARGE:** Once per scene, Eltric may **move two zones** in one exchange and gains **+2 to Fight or +2 to Physique** for the action declared before moving.

## Extras
### Elder Elk's Axe (Signature Weapon)
- **Aspect:** Wielder of Elder Elk's Axe
- **Notes:** Ancestral Stagborne weapon carved from Falkreath's eldest tree; bears the clan's legacy and expectations.

### The Thu'um (Shouts)
- **Unrelenting Force (Fus Ro Dah):** Blast targets back; create advantage *Blown Back / Off Balance* or overcome obstacles with raw force.
- **Kyne's Peace (Kaan Drem Ov):** Stillness of Kyne; calm beasts, de-escalate animal hostility, or create advantage *Peace of the Wild*.
- **Disarm (Zun Haal Viik):** Force enemies to drop weapons; create advantage *Disarmed* or overcome armed superiority without slaughter.

> Optional limiter (if the GM uses it): track a **Thu'um Strain** clock (0/3). Each shout ticks it by 1; at 3/3 you cannot shout again until a short rest/meditation scene.
